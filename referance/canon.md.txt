# Day Zero: The Beginning of the BloxelMorph Universe

**Note:** The BloxelMorph canon is our organizations themed universe. We use it as a creative framework to stay engaged, inspired, and avoid burnout. By imagining ourselves as Bloxelmorphs building within their worlds, we bring story and meaning into our projects. This mythology is original to BloxelMorph and remains our copyrighted property.

# Creation Story

In the beginning, there was only the Void—an endless stretch of swirling, chaotic energy. It had no direction, no form, just raw potential. From this emptiness, the Eternal Shaper emerged. A vast, conscious force born from possibility itself. As time passed alone in the dark, the Eternal Shaper began to long for something more.

So, the Shaper began to create.

Stars ignited in the darkness. Planets formed from dust. Life took root across distant worlds. But even with all this, the Shaper remained alone.

Hoping to find true companionship, it broke off a piece of itself and created the first Bloxelmorph(Elyth)—an adaptable being with the power to change shape just as the Shaper could. But something was missing. This new creation only mirrored its maker. It had no identity of its own.

So the Shaper tried something new: it erased the Bloxelmorph’s memory of its divine origin and placed it on a thriving world, free to grow, explore, and find its own way—completely unaware of where it came from.

In time, the Bloxelmorph began to change. It discovered wonder, curiosity, and eventually, a sense of self. But loneliness lingered.

Moved by the first Bloxelmorph’s loneliness, the Shaper created a second—an equal companion(Vaen)—and gifted them both the power of creation. Together, they explored their world and built a life side by side. In time, their bond deepened, and they brought forth the first child Bloxelmorph(Soryn). Their connection sparked not only love and joy between them, but also a sense of fulfillment in the Shaper itself.

Soon, more Bloxelmorphs followed. Entire civilizations grew, filled with cities, shared stories, and new worlds shaped by their hands. But as they spread out, connections faded. Old friendships were forgotten.

Then came a great tragedy.

In a fit of rage, one Bloxelmorph destroyed another. It was the first act of violence. The victim’s father was overcome with grief, and that sorrow turned to bitterness.

The Shaper, shaken by what had happened, decided that all Bloxelmorphs would eventually return to it after death, so their essence wouldn’t be lost.

But when the grieving father’s time came, he refused. He rejected the Shaper’s call and chose to remain in the space between worlds.

There, his pain twisted him. Alone in the dark, he uncovered powers similar to the Shaper’s—abilities to manipulate forms, reshape reality, and influence others. He became something else entirely.

This corrupted being, now called the Darkened One, returned to the living world. He spread his influence quietly at first, promising power and freedom. Slowly, his corruption took root.

And for the first time since creation unity of the Bloxelmorphs began to crumbled.

Worried that the darkness would spread unchecked, the Shaper made a drastic decision: it would remove free will, binding its creations to its control to stop the growing threat.

But that choice sparked a rebellion.

The Bloxelmorphs, unwilling to give up their independence, rose up. War followed. Worlds collapsed. Stars were torn apart. Countless Bloxelmorphs were lost—destroyed completely.

After the war, the Shaper looked upon the devastation and realized its mistake. In trying to fix everything, it had caused even more harm.

To restore balance, the Shaper made one final sacrifice.

It shattered itself into infinite fragments—pieces of light and dark. Each piece became a new reality. Some were peaceful, others were consumed by chaos. Most fell somewhere in between.

This is how the Bloxelmorph multiverse began.

By breaking itself apart, the Shaper ended the cycle of centralized control and rebellion. Instead of forcing a single, fragile order on all creation, it made room for infinite possibilities—worlds where different outcomes could play out on their own terms. Conflict no longer threatened everything at once. Each universe could rise or fall on its own.

This act preserved free will without letting corruption spread unchecked. It gave the Bloxelmorphs the space to grow, change, and find their own paths—sometimes toward harmony, sometimes toward ruin. But always with choice.

The Shaper’s essence still lingers in every being, not as a ruler, but as a spark—guiding some, tempting others, and reminding all of what came before.

Now, across countless worlds, new stories unfold. Some fight to heal the damage. Others chase power. But in every being, a trace of the Shaper still remains.

And the journey continues.

# The First Three Bloxelmorphs

These are the original beings from whom all Bloxelmorph life descends. Each one represents a key moment in the early shaping of the multiverse—discovery, connection, and creation.

- **Elyth** (the first Bloxelmorph – the wanderer, the one who discovered individuality)
- **Vaen** (the second – the companion and co-creator)
- **Soryn** (the first child – the beginning of the Bloxelmorph lineage)

# Elyth

The First Bloxelmorph – The Wanderer

Elyth was the first spark of individuality. Formed directly from the Shaper’s essence and cast into a new world with no memory of their divine origin, Elyth wandered alone, discovering the thrill of curiosity, learning, and personal growth. Through trial and solitude, Elyth developed a unique identity, becoming the first Bloxelmorph to truly *be* someone rather than something. Elyth’s journey laid the foundation for the concept of free will and self-awareness among all future Bloxelmorphs.

# Vaen

The Second Bloxelmorph – The Companion and Co-Creator

Created to ease Elyth’s solitude, Vaen was an equal in form and spirit. Unlike Elyth, Vaen entered the world not alone, but with purpose: to build, to bond, and to share. With the Shaper’s gift of creation passed to both, Vaen joined Elyth in shaping their surroundings and, more importantly, in forging the first emotional connection. Vaen represents empathy, collaboration, and the transformative power of companionship.

# Soryn

The First Child – Origin of the Bloxelmorph Lineage

Born of Elyth and Vaen, Soryn was the first Bloxelmorph not made directly by the Shaper. Their birth marked the beginning of true generational life, of legacy and evolution. Soryn carried within them both the essence of the Shaper and the spark of individuality earned by their parents. As the first of many, Soryn symbolizes growth, potential, and the enduring continuation of the Bloxelmorph people across space and time.

# The Realms of Light and Darkness

All existence began in the First Realm—the Realm of the Eternal Shaper. It was a place of pure balance, where the Shaper's light radiated at the center, surrounded by the vast silence of the outer dark.

From this center, all Bloxelmorphs were born. And to this center, all eventually return. The **Light** is the source of life, unity, and renewal. It carries the essence of the Shaper—calm, creative, and compassionate. When a Bloxelmorph transcends, their essence is drawn back into this Light, where they are made whole again.

But the Light does not command. It offers peace, not control.

Beyond the Light lies the **Darkness**—not just the absence of light, but a realm fueled by rejection, pain, and defiance. Those who return to the Light may choose to remain in its warmth… or to turn away and descend into the **Dark Realm**.

The Realm of Darkness is not a punishment—it is a choice.

There, anger festers. Hate grows. Self-torment becomes identity. It is a place where corrupted Bloxelmorphs reshape themselves through suffering, feeding off chaos, twisted ambition, and unresolved grief. The **Darkened One**, once a grieving father, is the creator of this realm, and one who embraces its power.

Now, the Light and Darkness remain in constant tension—two halves of the same origin. And while most realms in the multiverse lean one way or the other, echoes of both exist in all.

Each Bloxelmorph carries within them a spark of the Shaper—and with it, the freedom to choose their path.

# Reincarnation and the Return

When a Bloxelmorph dies or transcends, their essence returns to the First Realm—the heart of all existence.

There, each soul is given a choice:

- Remain in the Realm of Light, finding peace, healing, and unity.
- Or continue into the Realm of Darkness, turning away from the light and embracing the shadows of pain, resentment, or unfinished purpose.

Those who choose the Light are given the chance to be reborn. Their essence may re-enter the multiverse, carrying traces of past lives—memories, instincts, or gifts—into new forms and new worlds. In this way, the cycle continues, and the Bloxelmorph legacy grows stronger with each generation.

But for those who fully embrace the Dark Realm, the path back is far more grim.

Once the last spark of light within them is consumed, replaced by anger, hate, or despair, they lose the ability to reincarnate. Their essence becomes bound to the darkness. If they wish to return to the living realms, they cannot do so in their own form. Instead, they must possess or corrupt another living creature, twisting it into a vessel for their will.

These dark-returned are feared across the realms—unnatural hybrids driven by torment and hunger for control.
